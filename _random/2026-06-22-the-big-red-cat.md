# The Big Red Cat

Once upon a time, in a little town by the sea, there lived a cat.

But not just any cat.

**The Big Red Cat.**

He was as red as the reddest fire truck you ever saw. He was as big as a house — no, bigger than a house! His paws were as big as beach balls, and his tail could wrap around the whole school playground.

{% include image.html src="/assets/images/2026-06-22-the-big-red-cat-1.jpg" alt="The Big Red Cat playing fetch in the park with tiny people and small dogs looking up in wonder" %}

## Chapter 1: A Friend Like No Other

The Big Red Cat had a best friend named Lily.

Lily was small. Lily was brave. And Lily loved that cat more than all the stars in the sky.

Every morning, Lily would walk to the meadow where the Big Red Cat liked to stretch out in the sun. She would climb up his big red paw, sit right between his ears, and say:

*"Good morning, Big Red Cat! What shall we do today?"*

And the Big Red Cat would purr so loud that the flowers would dance.

## Chapter 2: The Problem with Being Big

Now, being the biggest cat in the whole wide world wasn't always easy.

The Big Red Cat couldn't fit inside the library. (He knocked over three bookshelves trying once.)

He couldn't go to the movie theater. (He sat on someone's car by accident.)

And he *definitely* couldn't fit inside Lily's house.

But that was okay. The Big Red Cat didn't mind sleeping outside. He would curl up in the backyard with his tail sticking out the window, and Lily would read him stories until he fell asleep.

{% include image.html src="/assets/images/2026-06-22-the-big-red-cat-2.jpg" alt="The Big Red Cat sleeping curled up in a cozy house too small for him, tail sticking out the window, Lily reading next to his paw" %}

## Chapter 3: The Apple Tree

One autumn day, Lily looked up at the tallest apple tree in the park.

"I wish I could reach those apples," she said. "They look so red and juicy."

The Big Red Cat smiled his big red smile.

He walked over to the tree, and Lily climbed up to sit right on top of his head. His fur was warm and soft, like sitting on the coziest pillow in the whole world.

Up, up, up she went!

"Pick away!" said the Big Red Cat.

And Lily picked apples until her basket was full, and then she picked some more for the Big Red Cat.

{% include image.html src="/assets/images/2026-06-22-the-big-red-cat-3.jpg" alt="The Big Red Cat helping Lily reach apples from a tall tree, she sits on his head picking apples with autumn leaves falling" %}

## Chapter 4: The Best Thing

That night, Lily and the Big Red Cat sat together under the stars.

"Big Red Cat," said Lily, "you are the best friend anyone could ever have."

The Big Red Cat purred.

He purred for Lily. He purred for the moon. He purred for the whole wide world.

And if you listen very carefully on a quiet night, you can still hear him purring.

**The End.**
